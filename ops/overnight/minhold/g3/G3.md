# 3GzBLpxT (1.5 SOL group), every position in our recordings (R1–R4, 10-22 → 10-24 22:00 PKT)
Scripts: build.py, analyze.py → analyze.txt (full table), strategy.json.
- **Coverage:** the group traded 77 tokens; 3GzB had 62 positions (20 on quote-token curves).
- **Closed on the curve: 35.** Cash PnL +7.54 SOL, 57% win.
- **Held past the end of our tapes: 27 bags,** ~1.0–1.55 SOL each (38.6 SOL cost). Only 3 completed the curve inside our tapes. The rest sat at 30–60 SOL curve; some were sold hours later on PumpSwap/routers (seen in 2pLu's chain txs).
- **Pattern:**
  - 4 wallets buy in one slot, 1.0 or 1.5 SOL each.
  - Token age from 7 s to 20 h (median a few minutes).
  - Copy bots add 8–19 SOL within 1 s every time; the price peaks at 1.6–2.2x within 0.1–0.5 s.
  - Their exits vary: 0.2–15 min, hours, or never on the curve.
- **Copy the group's first buy + our own exit** (1 SOL; choose on R1+R2 = 23 copies, test on R3+R4 = 39):

  | Variant | 35 ms | 50 ms | 75 ms |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | We pay vs the group | +43% | +63% | +70% |
  | Keep followers (optimistic), 1 s hold, train / test | +3.3 / +6.8 | +2.1 / +2.8 | +0.8 / +0.6 |
  | Drop big copy bots in the first slot (heuristic), train / test | +1.2 / +0.2 | +0.7 / −0.5 | +0.4 / −0.8 |
  | Drop all followers within 2 slots (pessimistic) | negative | negative | negative |

  - Best train rule at 35 ms (stall 0.5 s): +3.5 train → +6.95 test (optimistic only).
- **Verdict:** the copy bots on this group land in its own slot and take +43–70% of the move before 35–75 ms. Copying is ≈0 in the realistic case. Their edge is that their buy triggers the copy-bot wave.
