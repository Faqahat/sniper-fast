# Create-time filters: can we fire on more launches and still make money? (2026-10-21)

## Summary

- **Data.** I priced 932 launches on the realistic landing model. These are all the launches the engine watched (base filter + close leader) in 12 engine logs. Together the logs cover 5.8 h, from 10-20 12:40 to 10-21 10:13 UTC. The model is the unchanged `sim_land.py` model, using the landing time for each leader type, the real block order, the 85% price cap, all fees, and a sell at the end of S+2.
- **Rule #2 is profitable in every period.**
  - Evening, in-sample (A): 12.3 launches/h, **+0.133 SOL per launch**.
  - Older and overnight logs, out-of-sample (B): 9.1/h, **+0.058**.
  - This morning (C): 1.2/h, a single launch. The drought is caused by market volume, not the filter: base-filter launches are 217/h this morning against 581/h in the evening.
- **Lowering the bundle or taking single-dev launches does not add profitable volume.** Every one of these lost money in every period where it had launches:
  - Lunar with no bundle: 9 launches, −0.04 SOL each.
  - 1-wallet bundles: 27 launches.
  - Lunar 2–3 SOL bundles: −0.22 (A), −0.94 (B).
  - 0–1 SOL bundles.
  - 8+ SOL bundles.
  - Late creates (150–217 ms): −0.20 (A), −0.51 (B). The exception is one +2.75 outlier this morning.
- **Only one widening was positive in all three periods.** It drops the Lunar condition and takes bundles of 2–5 SOL (C6). That gives **2.7× the launches**: 33/h (A), 29/h (B), 11/h (C).
  - The ADDED launches made +0.012 / +0.014 / +0.083 SOL each.
  - That is positive but not distinguishable from zero. The out-of-sample 90% interval is −0.016 to +0.060.
  - If we land 20 ms later, the evening group's added launches turn negative (−0.82 SOL).
- **One surprise needs a paper test.** No-bundle launches on Harmonic leaders (C7) made +0.056 per launch in-sample (49) and +0.017 out-of-sample (72), but −0.05 this morning (5). It rests mostly on one log, the 1 h dry hour.
- **Recommendation.** Keep rule #2 live, unchanged. Put C6 (and optionally C7) on paper alongside it. Promote either only if paper confirms more than +0.02 SOL per added launch over at least 100 added launches. Confidence:
  - Medium that the bundle/single-dev relaxations lose.
  - Low on C6/C7 being better than break-even.

## How it was measured

- **Launches.** Every `RACE seen` launch in the logs, meaning base filter passed (dev 0.5–8 SOL, vanilla, SOL quote, uri blacklist) and the create-slot leader was on the close list (<5 ms). Far-leader launches have no feed timeline, so they cannot be priced this way.
  - Logs: dry_cov, dry_alt, dry_cad, live_edge1, dry_latest, live_edge2, the 1 h dry hour, Run 1 (prod_p1), live_edge3, live_edge5, the overnight dry_run1.prev, and the current dry_run1 (copied at 10:13 UTC).
  - 27 more launches were phantoms: the create failed on chain. None of them passed the race gate.
- **Periods.**
  - **A (in-sample, used to choose candidates):** the 1 h dry hour (10-20 19:50–20:50) and Run 1 (21:54–22:26), 1.55 h.
  - **B (out-of-sample):** all older and overnight logs, 3.40 h.
  - **C (out-of-sample):** the current paper log, 0.83 h, this morning.
  - Launches/h = launches ÷ the log's span (first to last slot × the log's PoH slot length).
- **Per launch inputs.**
  - Create fields from `RACE seen`.
  - The feed timeline of outside buys from `RACE obs outs`.
  - Create time in slot from `DETECT feed=erpc poh_ms`, the same number the 150 ms gate uses.
  - Blocks S..S+2 via public `getBlock` (exact order).
  - The creator's glued bundle: consecutive successful buys of the mint right after the create tx (votes not counted), with its SOL, legs and distinct wallets.
  - The create tx's largest top-level transfer to a non-signer, classed as follows:
    - `lunar` = moon…
    - jito / nozomi (including noz… vanity accounts)
    - other tip service (tip map)
    - fee/other address: mostly launch-platform fees (uxento `uxto…`, j7tracker `dev…`, `KfHbT…`)
    - none
- **Checks.**
  - The on-chain rule #2 reconstruction (Lunar largest, glued 3–5, create ≤150) matches the engine's FIRE lines: 48 of 48 fires, plus 3 boundary launches (bundle exactly 5.0 / 3.0) the engine did not fire.
  - The engine's `bundle_sol` matches the on-chain glued SOL within 0.1 for 186 of 186 decided launches.
  - The paper loop's first paper trade (6uXsdCEg, 10:12): this model gives +0.0800 S+2 / +0.0569 S+1, identical to paper_live.py.
- **Model.** `sim_land.py` `run()`, with the owner's economics unchanged:
  - Buy 1.5 SOL. Priority fee 0.011 always paid.
  - Tips only when filled: BAM route 0.015, Jito-client 0.004, Harmonic 0.001, other 0.004.
  - Pump fee 1.25% per side, sell tx 0.0007.
  - Cap: our tokens ≥ 85% of the post-bundle quote; a refused buy loses 0.011.
  - Landing (feed ms after the create): BAM 20, Jito-client 35, Harmonic 32, other 35 (Firedancer/Agave/Rakurai), plus a +20 ms column.
  - Exit: sell everything at the end of S+2 (S+1 also shown).
- **Two small changes** (both make the model more conservative or more correct):
  1. An outside buy our feed saw before our landing time is matched to the block by wallet prefix (payer or user) even when the ±2% size match fails. The feed's SOL for cap-type buys is an estimate that ignores the bundle. With strict matching, the total over all 932 launches is +5.87 SOL; with this matching it is +4.61.
  2. In the live logs, our own real buys (11 launches) are removed and the curve is replayed without them.
- **Leader client.**
  - Fired launches use the client from the engine's FAST-FIRE line.
  - Other launches use the 10-20 evening client map (the recorder's `leader_client`).
  - The current gossip is used for the morning log and for leaders not in that map.
  - **Caveat:** 42 leaders that the engine saw as `AgaveBam` on 10-20 now show `JitoLabs` in gossip on both public RPCs. 209 launches in A/B use the gossip class, so a few BAM launches may be priced as Jito-client (35 ms instead of 20; tip 0.004 instead of 0.015).
- **Columns.**
  - n.
  - /h = launches per hour.
  - fill/ref = filled / refused by the cap.
  - PnL S+2 = total SOL.
  - per launch = mean.
  - median.
  - win = share of launches with PnL > 0.
  - worst = worst single launch.
  - PnL S+1.
  - +20 ms S+2 = total if we land 20 ms later.
  - SOL/h S+2.
  - S+2 w/o best 3 = total without the 3 best launches, which shows how much depends on outliers.

  - Our landing time is the measured median per leader type. It is not measured for launches of a different shape (no-bundle, other tips).
  - The +20 ms column is the stress test. C7 and the A part of C6 are sensitive to it. Rule #2 is not.
- **Leader class (measured + inference).** BAM leaders changed their gossip client id overnight (see "Leader client" above).
- **Harmonic no-bundle (C7) (inference).** The edge, if real, likely comes from cheap single-route landing (tip 0.001) on launches nobody fights over. On launches with no buyers, a fill costs about 0.05 SOL in fees.
- **Not covered.** Far leaders (no feed timeline), and anything after the create (outs, buyer counts), since this study is create-time only.
- **Phantom launches (measured).** 27 creates failed on chain (2.8% of watched launches). None passed the race gate. A no-bundle filter would fire on some of them and lose the 0.011 priority fee each.
