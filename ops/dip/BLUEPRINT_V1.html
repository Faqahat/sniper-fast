1. The model in one page

What it does. On a young pump.fun token, when someone sells ≥ 1 SOL into the bonding curve (a dump), we buy 0.25 SOL right behind it (same or next slot) if a frozen model, trained to imitate three profitable dip bots, rates that dump in its top 2%. The buy is protected by a price guard (max 5% above the post-dump price) and a slot deadline (dump slot + 1). We sell on +5% take-profit, −10% stop-loss, or after 6 slots (~1.3 s), whichever comes first.

Why it works when it works. A sharp dump on a fresh, busy token gets bought back within ~1–2 s by a cohort of consistently profitable bots; we try to sit in front of them. When it does not. The fastest bots buy the bounce 2–40 ms after the dump; when they beat us, our guard refuses the higher price and we pay only the fee (~0.0005 SOL).

So far (live, 0.25 SOL): 29 buy attempts, 8 fills, 7 wins, chain P&L +0.2582 SOL (run 1 +0.1249 · run 2 +0.1333).

2. Why it makes money

Measured, not assumed (study logs: memory profit-source-bot-cohort, dip-census; ops/overnight/dip, ops/overnight/verify_dip):

  • The buyers after us are a pro cohort. Removing the later buys of 541 consistently winning wallets turns the dip replay at 35 ms from +18.07 to -14.11 SOL (same test with 541 random wallets removed: +11.36). Our edge = getting in ahead of them.
  • It is a stable pro niche. Corrected census, 33 h: 53 wallets with ≥ 100 dip trips made +189.8 SOL net, 79% of them positive; the skill persists across runs (R1-positive wallets +119.8 on R2, 85% positive).
  • Speed decides. Census by delay after the dump, closed trips: 0–10 ms +7.3%, 10–60 ms +0.9%…+2.0%, 60 ms+ ≤ 0.
  • Selection matters as much as speed. Unscored dumps win ~29% (dump-bounce test); the imitation model's picks win 66–89% in the backtest.

3. Pipeline, step by step

#stepexactly
1FeedExecuted transactions of the pump program from Constant-K Kaldera yellowstone-tx (gRPC, processed commitment, account filter = pump). Each tx carries its block index, fee, every pump TradeEvent (user, buy/sell, SOL, tokens, virtual reserves after, fee bps, creator-fee bps) and CreateEvent. Shred feeds (kaldera deshred, turbo-kaldera, erpc entries) run beside it for timing only. Measured: the executed feed is 1.6 ms behind the first sighting (p90 5.5 ms).
2Follow new tokensOn a CreateEvent the engine opens a token book (launch slot, index, creator, signer, mayhem flag) and decodes the create tx's accounts (bonding curve, creator vault, create_v2 flags). Only tokens created after the engine started are followed.
3Rebuild the tapeEvery TradeEvent of a followed token is appended in chain order (slot, index, event). Out-of-order trades are inserted in place; a gap (pre-state ≠ previous post-state on the constant product) gets a synthetic trade, exactly as the study's rp.repair_gaps.
4Launch structureGlued bundle = the create tx plus the unbroken run of buys right after it in the launch slot (index +0/+1). Insiders = creator, signer, glued buyers. g_end = first trade after the glued run.
5Dump detectionA trade is a dump when: it is a sell ≥ 1 SOL, not synthetic, after g_end, token age 1–1800 s (slot × 212 ms), curve vSOL < 100 SOL after it, and it is the first big sell of its slot.
6Launch filterSkip tokens that are mayhem, whose curve ever dropped under 25 SOL vSOL, or whose first 50 trades went above 200 SOL vSOL.
7EnvelopeInside the 3 bots' envelope only: token age ≤ 343.67 s, price drop ≥ 4.65%, last buy ≤ 3.869 s before the dump, dump ≥ 1.08 SOL.
8Score42 features (section 4) → IMIT1 GBDT (400 trees). PICK when score ≥ 0.5419 (the model's top 2% = “strict”).
9Pick checksSame token: ≥ 5 slots since the last pick (spacing). Token kind: create_v2 only (legacy create skipped); vanilla or holder-reward (cashback, creator-fee, mayhem skipped). One open position at a time. No new buys after the kill switch, the run's buy cap or its time cap. Balance ≥ size + 0.03 SOL.
10Guard + deadlinemin tokens out = the post-dump curve's quote for our spend ÷ 1.05 (pump error 6042 if the price is > 5% higher when we execute). A Lighthouse assertSysvarClock instruction fails the tx if it lands after dump slot + 1 (error 6001).
11SendPre-built, pre-signed v0 tx (tip transfers, CU limit 100,000, CU price 5,000,000 µL/CU, create ATA, guard, buy_exact_quote_in_v3 0.25 SOL) written on the engine's own warm HTTP/UDP connections to the two fastest paths for the slot's leader client (section 5). Decision → first write measured 0.7–1.7 ms.
12LandOur TradeEvent on the executed feed (or the RPC land poll) opens the position at the real token count and cost.
13ExitAfter every later trade on the token: value = curve sell-out of our tokens after fees vs cost. ≥ +5% → sell (take-profit); ≤ −10% → sell (stop-loss); else sell when the slot reaches landing + 5 (lands at +6 ≈ 1.3 s). Wall-clock backstop: 6 × 400 ms + 3 s after the fire.
14Sellsell_v3 all tokens + close the token account (rent back), to the staked RPC and Nozomi together (one signature), retried each second until a confirmed zero balance, 30 s deadline.
15Record + reportJSONL per event (dump, pick, fire with decision breakdown, buy_land, exit, sell_land, tape of every trade while we hold, close, timing). report_run.py turns it into the per-trade report with chain P&L.

6. Exit and sell

  • Take-profit +5% / stop-loss −10%, checked after every trade on the token from the executed feed (value = curve sell-out of our tokens, after pump fees, vs our cost incl. 0.0008 per tx).
  • Time limit 6 slots (~1.3 s): the sell is sent one slot before, when slot ≥ landing + 5. Taken from HkQ7hMjy's own timer: it sells 25–35% of open trips at slot 6, never before slot 3.
  • Why not longer: on the 12.3 h TEST the 192-exit grid is flat around +6.6…+7.0 and longer holds earn less; the live paper liked 6–12 s holds (no TP/SL 12 s +5.2 vs +2.2 at 0.5 SOL) but the backtest did not confirm it.
  • Sell: sell_v3 of all tokens + close the token account, sent to staked RPC and Nozomi together (audit rule M-5: every sell also via the fallback, 0.001 tip), retried every 1 s, until a confirmed zero balance; 30 s deadline then a background retry. Every run so far: 0 tokens left over.

Where the time goes (dip8 timing records)

  • Executed feed vs first sighting on any feed: median 1.6 ms (p90 5.5 ms).
  • Our decision, first sighting → buy written: 0.7–1.7 ms (queue 36–298 µs, book ~30 µs, score 65–844 µs, checks + logs 135–525 µs, build + sign 123–569 µs, write 74–160 µs).

12. Caveats (exact)

  • Our fail ratio is far above our competitors'. PROD: 21 of 29 buy attempts missed (72%: guard 20, deadline 1); FRZ: 11 of 12. haqqiUUX: 114 of its last 1,000 txs failed (11.4%; about 25% of its buy attempts, estimated from a 40-tx sample: 16 buys / 24 sells). DYXDDrZC: 42,972 failed attempts in 24 h.
  • Speed gap: we land 60 ms (nearby, median) / 113 ms (far) after the dump; the bounce bots 2–40 ms. We fill mostly when nobody bounces first.
  • Tiny sample: 8 fills. The backtest at our real ~110 ms is thin: +0.496 SOL per 12.3 h at 0.25 SOL (82 fills of 407 picks).
  • Profit depends on the bot cohort buying after us; without it the same replay loses (−14.11 vs +18.07).
  • Costs per round trip: buy fee 0.000505 + 2 tips 0.002 (3 when the leader changes) + sell fee 0.000605 + sell tip 0.001 + pump fees ~1.25% each way. At 0.25 SOL that is ~1.6% + 2.5%.
  • Model drift: trained on 10-22/23 data; the 3 bots can change behaviour; no retraining loop yet.
  • One region (Frankfurt): 26% of slots belong to leaders ≥ 50 ms away (167–195 ms landings).
  • Timing field bug: dip8's PoH “leader clock” value is wrong (our entries feed skips most ticks) — ignore land_ms_poh; use fire_to_seen_ms.
  • Sizes above 0.25 SOL are untested live (price impact on our own fill, follower limits).
