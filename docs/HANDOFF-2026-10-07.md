  85% of failed bundles never reach an auction (Jito's simulation finds the gap gone).
- fastL1 landings (20): send->landed (first seen in DZ shreds) median 202 ms (19-1,065). **Jito-relayer leaders 59 ms
  median, BAM leaders 176 ms.** Astralane fastest (19, 26, 53 ms). All 20 lost; 2 of the first 3 were phantoms (the
  trigger swap itself failed: 6001 / 3012 slippage).
- Landing blocks: positions are NOT ordered by priority fee (Spearman ~0); our CU price ranks ~85th percentile. Takers
  of our gaps: 59% priority-fee only (median 50,000 lamports), Jito tips (p50 8,204, up to 0.2 SOL), Astralane 0.001 SOL.
  So fee is not why we are late; arrival time is. (The 30% priority split was kept at the owner's request for fastL2.)
- Pending-only funnel (fastL1, per minute): 823 decoded swaps, 393 skipped (failing signers), 361 priced, ~10
  opportunities; 2,392 commits/min skipped: most opportunities come from router swaps (Jupiter/DFlow/OKX) we cannot
  decode from shreds.
- DoubleZero on Cherry: tunnel up, first source of ~83% of transactions, 0 UDP drops. The shred blockhash can lag when a
  slot is incomplete (seen 232 slots once).
