# DOC1 — MEViEnsc (SolanaMevBot, "Program 2"): claim-by-claim verification

**Claims:** 285. **Final verdicts:** confirmed 169, opinion-supported 46, partly 41, wrong 20, opinion-unsupported 7, unverifiable 2.

**How each verdict was reached:**
- Every claim was checked by a first verifier (V1).
- 94 disputed claims were re-checked by a second verifier (V2), which tried to prove the doc right.
- 42 claims where V1 and V2 disagreed (of 42) were decided by a judge, who ran their own decisive checks.
- The "(source)" in the verdict column says which step produced the final verdict.

## Summary

# Doc1 (MEViEnsc / SolanaMevBot): verification summary

285 claims were checked. Each final verdict is V1's, or V2's where the two disagreed, with 42 adjudications applied on top. The adjudications kept the earlier verdict in every case except D1-071 and D1-251, which moved from confirmed to partly.

## Verdict counts
- Confirmed 169 (59%), opinion-supported 46 (16%), partly 41 (14%), wrong 20 (7%), opinion-unsupported 7 (2%), unverifiable 2 (1%).
- 221 factual claims: 169 confirmed (76%), 34 partly, 16 wrong, 2 unverifiable.
- 64 interpretations and recommendations: 46 supported, 7 partly, 7 unsupported, 4 wrong.

## Trustworthiness by part
- **Binary/bytecode facts (162: 130 confirmed, 17 partly, 15 wrong).**
  - The raw measurements reproduce exactly:
    - 192,275 instructions and 614 functions.
    - 533 panic Locations, with the per-file counts.
    - 34 invoke_signed_c sites and 22 try_find_program_address sites.
    - The entrypoint chain, and the per-function sizes and mul/div counts.
    - Upgrade authority SMB6JY and its 3 in-code compares.
    - HumidiFi's hardcoded ID, the Pinocchio framework, and the 14.0x instruction ratio to Babq.
  - The errors are about identification: what a function is, which venue a module targets, and what counts as a loop or a discriminator.
  - Five of the 15 wrong verdicts have one root cause: the "pending" venue IDs.
- **On-chain behaviour (55: 36 confirmed, 17 partly, 1 wrong, 1 unverifiable).**
  - The overall picture holds:
    - About 98.5% of transactions are clean no-op quotes, mostly from 4vAqkstz.
    - Real trades are rare 2-leg routes over DLMM, PumpSwap and DAMMv2.
    - Failures are near zero, against about 85% for Babq.
  - The doc does not state its sample windows. Once they were found, its rates and shares reproduce:
    - 1,000-tx window: 00:10:45-00:22:06Z, 88 tx/min.
    - 400-tx window: 00:48:07-00:51:30Z, 118 tx/min, with 394 no-ops, 5 trades and 1 failure.
  - The partly verdicts come from labels and ranges, not invented numbers.
- **Interpretations and recommendations (64).**
  - All 9 recommendations are opinion-supported.
  - The weak spots are causal stories and conclusions that rest on false facts:
    - D1-265 says MEVi's wins are fatter because it skips marginal trades. In fact it executes marginal trades.
    - D1-213 says the callers are one operator's hot wallets. They look like fee-paying customers of a public service.
    - D1-275 rests on the false D1-274.
  - D1-272 (keep an atomic profit guard) is still sound advice, but MEVi already has one.

## Most consequential errors (with corrected facts)
1. **"No post-trade check, no failures" (D1-274, D1-242 wrong; D1-269, D1-194/195 partly).** MEVi snapshots the settlement balance, re-reads it after the swaps, and reverts with Custom(1) unless the gain exceeds min_profit. This holds in both v9 and v10. Failures do occur: 108 of 23,533 landed txs (0.46%), some after swap legs had run.
2. **Raydium modules swapped (D1-069, D1-101 wrong; D1-075, D1-071 partly).** dex/raydium.rs targets Raydium AMM v4 (675kPX..., SwapBaseIn tag 9). dex/clmm.rs is the Raydium CLMM adapter: it checks for CAMM... and makes swap/swap_v2 CPIs. In v10 it also serves the PancakeSwap and Byreal forks. It is not a math layer without a program ID.
3. **"Pending" venue IDs (D1-092/094/096/098/100, D1-105 wrong; D1-057, D1-103 partly).** All 16 venue IDs are hardcoded. Byreal and Futarchy are stored as contiguous .rodata copies; Heaven, Vertigo and BisonFi exist only as lddw immediates. Jupiter's registry labels four of the five, and Vertigo's SDK names the fifth.
4. **Pump adapter (D1-081 partly).** It trades PumpSwap only. Pump.fun's ID 6EF8... is used only to derive the "pool-authority" PDA.
5. **Misidentified code (D1-201 wrong; D1-160-164, D1-200 partly).** 0x165cf0 is the unrolled SHA-256 compression function, not decode.rs: it holds all 64 round constants and has one outer loop over blocks. lib.rs, encode.rs, decode.rs, internal.rs and syscalls.rs are dependency code (base58, bytemuck, the SDK), not program glue.
6. **Dispatch and errors (D1-196, D1-030, D1-031 wrong; D1-282, D1-029 partly).** The 32-byte compares are program-ID gates: MEViEnsc and MEMfqXbT get full dispatch, BPodLBws only tag 0x3d. Instructions dispatch on a 1-byte tag. There are 26 error variants (0x00-0x19), converted by a binary-search tree, not 13 variants through a jump table.
7. **Loop counts (D1-183, D1-177 wrong; D1-173 partly).** The doc counted objdump jump lines containing "-0x". The real natural-loop counts are:
   - optimal_input: 2, not 165.
   - inverse: 14, not 115.
   - search: 21 (the doc's 293 is the number of backward jumps).
8. **Size (D1-012 wrong, D1-009 partly).** 2.76 MB is the size of the programData account and of the dumped file. The ELF itself is 1.66 MB, followed by about 1.1 MB of zero padding. The "25x" divides the padded size by Babq's .text size. Compared like for like, MEVi is about 14x Babq, or 19x account to account.
9. **SMB "service mode" (D1-214 wrong; D1-203, D1-283 partly).** No such mode exists. SMB6JY is an admin signer for the vault instructions 0x11-0x13. On each trade, the program pays a vendor fee collector.
10. **Babq comparisons (D1-036, D1-035 wrong; D1-168 partly).**
    - Babq has no per-venue adapters: one serializer with 13 arms CPIs into at least 14 venues.
    - MEVi has 15 dex modules, not 16.
    - Babq's sizer is a Newton square root with a golden-section search, not bisection, and it was never used live (0 of 293 txs).
11. **Architecture (D1-117, D1-139 partly; D1-184 unsupported).** arbitrage.rs is a mid-level v9 route executor. The math_v9 solvers are iterative (bisection over ported Raydium math), not closed-form, and cover only some venue pairs. optimal_input is used only by v9.
12. **On-chain labels (D1-220/221/251, D1-231/257, D1-233, D1-235 partly).**
    - The "land rate" is really the success share among landed txs.
    - Only about 29% of the 400-tx window landed in Jito bundles.
    - Lookup tables appear only in the v0 transactions, about 2% of the total.
    - A miss costs a median of about 8.5-8.9k lamports, not just the base fee.

## Could not be checked
- **D1-002, platform-tools v1.54.** Only v1.57 is installed. Every figure reproduces with v1.57, so this does not matter.
- **D1-216, the 1,000-tx window.** The doc gives no slots and keeps no raw data. A window whose statistics match was later found (D1-218), but that identification is inferred.
- **Gaps inside partly verdicts:**
  - Exact CLMM reproduction: no CLMM-leg trade was sampled, no quotes are logged, and CLMM CPIs pass 0 minimum-out.
  - The product_prefix math is inferred from the file name.
  - The true land rate cannot be seen on chain.
  - Jito explorer "not found" results are not proof, because the explorer has coverage gaps.

## Error patterns
- **Account size used as ELF size** (D1-009, D1-012).
- **Landed-only metrics called land rates** (D1-220/221/251).
- **Window and convention mismatches.**
  - Most disputes went away once the doc's earlier windows were found (D1-218, D1-249-253 now confirmed).
  - V1 had sampled around 02:xx, when no-op CU was about 81k against about 54k earlier (D1-254).
  - One-function vs whole-file counts (D1-176/180/181) were judged by the doc's consistent one-function convention.
- **Text patterns used in place of real structure.** "-0x" lines were counted as loops, a root pivot was read as "13 variants", and program-ID compares were read as discriminators.
- **Panic-path names taken at face value** without checking the code: the glue files, SHA-256, raydium/clmm, pump.
- **IDs left unresolved, and keys missed by byte search.**
  - The doc left publicly labelled IDs as "pending", even though two are stored contiguously.
  - A raw byte search cannot see keys built from lddw immediates. That blind spot hit the shared evidence base, not the doc: it wrongly called HumidiFi and SMB6JY absent. The verifiers overturned this, and the doc was right (D1-007, D1-090, D1-202).
- **Absolute claims from small samples.**
  - "No failed txs".
  - "All 4vAqkstz txs are no-ops" (D1-210).
  - Profit "+0.009 to +0.019 SOL", when the window shows -0.0015 to +0.084 (D1-262).
  - Trade CU "135-198k", when the actual range is 117-231k (D1-228).
